Farfalle al Pesto

A delicious Farfalle al Pesto dish

Farfalle al Pesto with cherry tomatoes and lettuce is a vibrant, refreshing twist on traditional Italian pasta cuisine that makes it an excellent option for hot summer days.

Ingredients

  • 225g (8oz) Farfalle
  • 1/2 cup basil pesto
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes
  • 1 cup lettuce
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • Salt and black pepper (to taste)
  • Grated Parmesan cheese (optional, for serving)

Steps

  1. Boil water on a large pot and add salt. Cook the farfatelle according to package instructions until al dente. Drain, reserving 2 tablespoons of pasta water.
  2. Return the warm pasta to the pot. Add the pesto and the pasta water. Toss gently until the pasta is evenly coated.
  3. Let the pasta cool slightly, then fold in the halved cherry tomatoes and the crisp lettuce.
  4. Drizzle with olive oil, season with salt and pepper to taste, and finish with grated Parmesan cheese if desired. Ready to serve.
